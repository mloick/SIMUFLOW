Replay your processes before you put them in place.

SimuFlow reads a process diagram (draw.io, BPMN, Visio, Excel, plain text, or a photo of a table), replays it step by step and shows you what is wrong: dead ends, isolated steps, paths that never finish. Then each actor validates their part. An iPhone and Android app, also usable in the browser, in English and French.

What the app does

Import

A draw.io, BPMN 2.0, Visio, JSON, Excel or CSV file, whatever the drawing style, or a process written as text, one line per step.

Scan

Take a photo of a table (step, actor, time limit): the text is read on the phone, without the Internet.

Replay

Step by step, simple case, complex case, or every path with the critical path. Parallel tasks move forward at the same time.

Correct

Rename, add, delete. The app remembers your corrections for the next imports.

Follow a real request

A deadline for each step in working days, a reminder on the morning of the due date, and a time limits mode (SLA / OLA) with timers and a final report.

Validate as a team

Group the processes of an initiative in a project and invite the team: each actor reviews and validates their part, even without an account thanks to a link. A dashboard shows how far the review has got.

Export

As PDF, draw.io, BPMN 2.0 or JSON, anonymised if needed, to share the validated diagram.

Who is it for?

Any project manager and their team, in any sector (IT, logistics, HR, purchasing, finance, customer service…), who write processes and want to check that they hold up, then have each actor validate them, before rolling them out.

Your data

Without an account, everything stays on your phone. With an account, your processes are stored on a server in the European Union (Paris), visible to you alone, and deleted with your account from the app. No ads, no audience tracking. Read the privacy policy.
