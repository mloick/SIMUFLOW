Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Applies to the SimuFlow app for iPhone and Android, and to its web version, version 1.0. This is a translation of the French policy, which prevails in case of difference.

In short

  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account, only your e-mail address, your profile, your processes, your projects, your reading rules, your saved forms and the review feedback are sent to the server, hosted in the European Union (Paris).
  • Only you can access them, except what you choose to share: a shared process can be read (never changed) by the people you invite, and a project by its team.
  • If you are sent a link to review your part without an account, only the name you type and your feedback are saved.
  • Scanner photos are read on the phone and are sent nowhere.
  • No ads, no audience tracking, no data sold or shared.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app, at any time.
  • In the web version, “phone” means your browser: without an account, your data stays in its local storage, and the session is kept in the same place. The Scanner reads the photo in the browser itself, with a reading engine served by this site: the image is sent nowhere. Reminders are not available there.

Who is responsible

SimuFlow is a personal project, developed by Loïck. For any question or request about your data, open an issue on the project page.

The data saved on the server is protected by access rules: each account sees only its own data and what has been shared with it, a member can never change a shared process, and each person can only give feedback on their own part. To invite someone by username or e-mail address, the server returns only their name and username: the app never sees the list of accounts or their addresses. Without signing in, nothing can be read, except the part entrusted to an actor link, by whoever has that link.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: asked the first time the Scanner opens, only to photograph a table. No microphone, no photo library.
  • Notifications: asked at the first deadline reminder or the first sharing alert. They are created by the phone itself, from what it receives when it syncs; no server sends them to you. You can turn them off in Settings.

Services used

  • Supabase (hosting of the server and the accounts, Paris region): receives the account data described above.
  • Expo (EAS Update): at launch, the app asks whether an update exists. This request contains technical information (app version, phone system), not your data.

No other service receives data from the app.

How long

Account data is kept as long as the account exists. An actor link no longer opens after 30 days (the owner can extend it); the feedback given through that link stays visible to the owner and the team until the link or the process is deleted. On the phone, it is kept until you sign out (the account's copies are then removed) or uninstall the app.

Your rights

At any time you can view your processes in the app, change them, delete them, and delete your account: Settings, then your profile, then “Delete my account”. The account, your profile, your processes (with their shares, links and the feedback received), your rules, your forms and the projects you manage are then erased from the server and the phone, as well as the feedback you gave on other people's processes. Processes that collaborators had put in your projects remain theirs. For any other request (access, rectification, objection), write via the project page. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated, and the change is visible in the history of the source code.
